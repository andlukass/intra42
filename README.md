# 42go

42go is an iOS and Android app for students at 42. It uses your 42 account to access the school's intranet.

## Features

- **Home** shows your profile, upcoming events, achievements, and time logged on campus.
- **Projects** shows your current projects, the project catalog, and the Holy Graph, a map of the curriculum.
- **Slots** lets you view, open, and cancel your availability for peer evaluations.
- **Clusters** shows campus maps and available workstations.
- **Search** finds students and projects and opens their profiles or details.
